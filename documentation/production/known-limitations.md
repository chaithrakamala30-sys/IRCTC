# Known Limitations & Operating Boundaries

1. **Demonstration Banking (Dhruva Bank)**:
   - All payment settlements and refunds are simulated on the demo platform.
   - Real payment processing requires commercial agreements with licensed payment aggregators.
2. **Free-Tier Host Latency (Render)**:
   - Backend spins down after 15 minutes of inactivity; first request encounters a 30–45s cold start.
3. **Independent Simulation Architecture**:
   - RailConnect is independently operated; it does not issue official IRCTC tickets or integrate with Indian Railways live tracking.
