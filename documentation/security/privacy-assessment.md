# Privacy & Data Protection Assessment

## 1. Regulatory Context
Evaluated against general data minimization principles and Indian Digital Personal Data Protection (DPDP) Act benchmarks.

## 2. Personal Identifiable Information (PII) Inventory
- **Collected**: Full Name, Email Address, Mobile Number, Passenger Age, Gender, PNR Numbers.
- **Excluded**: No payment card CVVs, full card numbers, or bank passwords are ever collected or stored.
- **Access Restrictions**: Passenger personal data is viewable solely by the passenger themselves and authorized support staff handling an active dispute. General analytical CSV exports exclude passenger names and phone numbers.
