# Compliance Readiness Assessment

## 1. Regulatory Context
- Evaluated against India's **Digital Personal Data Protection (DPDP) Act, 2023** principles.

## 2. Assessment Dimensions
1. **Notice & Consent**: Transparent privacy notice detailing what data is captured (Name, Mobile, Email, PNR) and the operational basis for processing.
2. **Data Minimization**: Financial secrets (card numbers, CVVs, net banking MPINs) are never captured or stored.
3. **Consumer Transparency**: Clear disclosures explicitly state that RailConnect is an independent simulation and reservation platform, distinct from official IRCTC/Indian Railways systems.
4. **Demonstration Banking Disclaimer**: Prominent disclaimers indicate all balances on Dhruva Bank are simulated demonstration credits.
